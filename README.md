## About This Course

Building Information Modelling (BIM) is the intelligent 3D model-based process at the heart of the modern built-environment industry — one shared, data-rich model that carries a project from planning and design through construction to operations. This 2-day SkillsFuture WSQ course teaches you to **apply BIM using Autodesk Revit**: model building elements parametrically, integrate architectural and engineering design, run analyses and quantity takeoffs, and maintain the BIM database and documentation to Singapore's BCA e-submission standards.

The course is hands-on throughout — ten guided Revit labs with real project datasets — and is aligned to the Skills Framework for the Built Environment TSC **Building Information Modelling Application (BEV-TEM-3027-1.1-1)**. It suits architects, engineers, drafters, contractors, facility managers and anyone moving from 2D CAD into BIM.

## Course Topics & Labs

| Topic | Coverage | Hands-On Labs |
|---|---|---|
| **1 · Introduction to BIM and Revit** | What is BIM · principles & dimensions (3D–8D) · value proposition · OIR/AIR/EIR/BEP · Revit for BIM | [Lab 1 — Get Started with Revit](labs/lab-01-get-started-with-revit/) · [Lab 2 — Convert 2D Information into a 3D Model](labs/lab-02-convert-2d-information-into-a-3d-model/) |
| **2 · BIM Modeling** | Walls, curtain walls, floors, ceilings, roofs, stairs · structure · site & topography · massing & Dynamo · families & materials | [Lab 3 — Modeling Walls](labs/lab-03-modeling-walls-reveals-embedded-and-curtain-walls/) · [Lab 4 — Floors, Ceilings, Roofs and Openings](labs/lab-04-floors-ceilings-roofs-and-openings/) · [Lab 5 — Stairs, Railings and Structural Elements](labs/lab-05-stairs-railings-and-structural-elements/) · [Lab 6 — Terrain, Building Pad and Conceptual Mass](labs/lab-06-terrain-building-pad-and-conceptual-mass/) |
| **3 · BIM Application** | Operating BIM · view templates · schedules & quantity takeoffs · cost estimates · energy analysis · compliance checks | [Lab 7 — View Templates, Schedules and Quantity Takeoffs](labs/lab-07-view-templates-schedules-and-quantity-takeoffs/) · [Lab 8 — Energy Analysis and Design Compliance Checks](labs/lab-08-energy-analysis-and-design-compliance-checks/) |
| **4 · BIM Documentation** | BEP/EIR/MIDP/CDE · drawings & specs · Singapore standards (SVY21, naming, colours) · worksharing · BCA e-submission | [Lab 9 — Sheets, Worksharing and Linked Models](labs/lab-09-sheets-worksharing-and-linked-models/) · [Lab 10 — BIM e-Submission Standards Check](labs/lab-10-bim-e-submission-standards-check/) |

Each lab has its own folder under [labs/](labs/) with a step-by-step README and the Revit dataset files (`.rvt`) it uses.

## Building the Courseware

The slide deck, Lesson Plan, Learner Guide and labs are generated from a single content source (`course_data.py` + `data_domain1..4.py` + `data_teaching.py`) so they can never drift apart:

```bash

python3 .claude/skills/courseware-build/build/build_labs.py      # labs/ folders
```
